## What Is a Vascular Ultrasound?

A vascular ultrasound is a non-invasive imaging test used to evaluate blood flow through arteries and veins. It uses high-frequency sound waves to create real-time images of blood vessels and detect abnormalities such as blockages, narrowing, blood clots, or weakened vessel walls. This test helps physicians assess circulation and identify vascular conditions early—often before symptoms become severe.

## When Is a Vascular Ultrasound Recommended?

A vascular ultrasound may be recommended if a patient experiences symptoms or has risk factors related to poor circulation or vascular disease, including:

- Leg pain, swelling, or cramping
- Suspected blood clots (deep vein thrombosis)
- Varicose veins or chronic venous insufficiency
- Carotid artery disease or stroke risk
- Peripheral artery disease (PAD)
- Monitoring known vascular conditions
- Follow-up after vascular procedures

## How Is a Vascular Ultrasound Performed?

During the procedure, a trained technologist applies a gel to the skin over the area being examined. A handheld device called a transducer is gently moved across the skin to capture images and measure blood flow. The test is painless, does not involve radiation, and typically takes 30 to 60 minutes to complete.

## What to Expect During Recovery After a Vascular Ultrasound?

There is no recovery period after a vascular ultrasound. Patients can resume normal activities immediately after the exam. No sedation or special aftercare is required, making it a convenient diagnostic option.

## What Are the Risks of a Vascular Ultrasound?

Vascular ultrasound is considered very safe. Potential risks are minimal and may include:

- Mild skin irritation from the gel
- Temporary discomfort from probe pressure
- There is no exposure to radiation or contrast dye.

## What Are the Benefits of a Vascular Ultrasound?

This diagnostic test offers several important benefits, including:

- Non-invasive and painless evaluation
- Early detection of vascular disease
- Real-time assessment of blood flow
- No radiation exposure
- Helps guide treatment decisions and prevent complications

Vascular ultrasound plays a key role in diagnosing and managing vascular health safely and effectively.
