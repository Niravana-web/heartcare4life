## Dr. Vimal Nanavati Board Certified in Cardiology and
Interventional Cardiology

Dr. Vimal Nanavati is board certified in cardiology and interventional cardiology and is an interventional invasive cardiologist based in Redding, California. He was recruited to Northern California from Ohio in 1997 to initiate a high-quality cardiology program serving the people of the Northern Coast of California. Dr. Nanavati is committed to delivering evidence-based, patient-centered cardiovascular care and focuses on advanced diagnostic and interventional techniques to improve long-term heart health.

Dr. Nanavati provides comprehensive cardiovascular care, offering both preventive and diagnostic services to identify heart conditions early and manage them effectively. His approach emphasizes careful evaluation, accurate diagnosis, and individualized treatment planning to address each patient’s unique cardiovascular needs.

With extensive experience in interventional cardiology, Dr. Nanavati performs minimally invasive procedures designed to restore blood flow, relieve symptoms, and reduce the risk of future cardiac events. He integrates advanced technology with proven techniques to deliver safe, effective care while prioritizing patient comfort and recovery.

Committed to long-term heart health, Dr. Nanavati partners closely with patients to support lifestyle modification, risk factor management, and ongoing monitoring. Through education, follow-up care, and evidence-based treatment strategies, he helps patients take an active role in maintaining and improving their cardiovascular health.
